The Program

Four Disciplines. Endless Progress.

Classes rotate in a continuous loop — a different training focus every day of the week.

⚡

HIIT

High-intensity interval training to maximise calorie burn, cardiovascular fitness, and metabolic conditioning.

🏋️

Strength

Progressive resistance training focused on building functional muscle and real-world strength.

💥

Power

Explosive plyometric and athletic sessions to develop power output and neuromuscular performance.

🧘

Flexibility & Mobility

Structured stretching and joint mobility work to improve range of motion and aid recovery.

Classes run in a continuous rotating loop — so every session hits a different training focus.
